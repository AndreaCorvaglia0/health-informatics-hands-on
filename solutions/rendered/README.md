# Rendered solution notebooks (with outputs)

Executed copies of three solution notebooks, to see what the plots and numbers look like
without running anything. They were produced on a **CPU**, in full (non-FAST) mode, on a
**small real subset**: 89 LUNA16 patients (subset0 only, re-dealt into 10 pseudo-folds:
63 train / 18 validation / 8 test). Numbers are therefore indicative only (baseline:
validation Dice 0.968, test Dice 0.971; notebook 03 took 13 minutes on 2 CPU cores).
